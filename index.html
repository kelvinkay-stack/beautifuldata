How to watch

Reading a bar chart race

A ranking table only tells you who's on top today. A race shows you how they got there — the slow climbs, the sudden collapses and the exact moment one contender passes another.

Bars re-rank in real time

Each bar is one contender. When one overtakes another it flashes, so the passes are impossible to miss.

Diamonds mark the big moments

Milestones on the timeline pop up a short story as the playhead crosses them. Click one to jump straight there.
