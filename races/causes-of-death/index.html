Leading Causes of Death in the US

Recorded deaths per year in the United States by underlying cause, 1950–2023, in thousands. Heart disease and cancer hold the top two places throughout, while the rest of the board reflects medicine and history: tuberculosis and infant conditions fading, HIV's rise and fall in the 1990s, the growth of overdose-driven injury deaths, and COVID-19 in 2020–2022.
