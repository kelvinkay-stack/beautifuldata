World's Largest Banks

Balance sheets of the world's biggest banks from 2004 to 2025, in US dollars. European megabanks balloon into the financial crisis, and RBS, swollen by its ABN AMRO takeover, is briefly the largest bank on Earth. Then China's state-owned Big Four overtake everyone: ICBC has been #1 since 2012 and now holds more than $7.6 trillion.
