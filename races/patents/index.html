Patent Applications by Office

How many patent applications each of the world's patent offices receives every year, from residents and foreigners alike. Japan's office led for decades until the USPTO caught it in 2006, then China's office went from barely existing in 1985 to No. 1 in 2011 and roughly three times the USPTO by 2024.
