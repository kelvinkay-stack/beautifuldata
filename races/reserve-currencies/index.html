Global Reserve Currencies

What the world's central banks hold their foreign-exchange reserves in, 1995–2025. The dollar never loses first place, sliding from 71% in 1999 to under 57% by 2025, so the race is for second place and below: the euro takes over the Deutsche mark's slot and the pound and yen trade #3. Then come the renminbi, the loonie, the Aussie dollar and a growing pile of 'other' currencies.
